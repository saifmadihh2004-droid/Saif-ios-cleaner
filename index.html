Automatic cleanup tips

iPhone does not let third-party apps erase other apps’ cache or clean system memory. These are safe actions you can do in Settings:

• Settings → General → iPhone Storage\n• Offload unused apps to keep their documents\n• Settings → Safari → Clear History and Website Data\n• Photos → Albums → Duplicates (if available)\n• Review large videos and downloads

Privacy: this mini-app scans only files you select. It does not upload your photos, access other apps, or delete anything automatically. iOS requires your confirmation for library changes.
